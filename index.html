How It Works

Create & Specify Need

Sign up with your UCU student/staff credentials and specify what you want to learn or teach (e.g. Piano, C++, Research).

Instant Matching Algorithm

Our engine compares availability, faculty background, and verification status to present top-rated mentors.

Session & Feedback Loop

Book 1-on-1 or group sessions, complete your milestone projects, and leave feedback to keep the community trusted

About Us

UCUSkillConnect is a peer-to-peer learning platform designed for Uganda Christian University students. We bridge the gap between academic theory and practical mastery by connecting students with skilled peer trainers across various faculties for personalized 1-on-1 coaching, coding labs, music lessons, and academic support.
